How to Tell You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • An outlet, a light fixture or the ceiling above has water dripping out of it.
  • The house was flooded when you walked in, with no telling how many hours it had been going.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Going away longer than a few days? Close the main water valve first.
  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season and consider a battery backup.
  • Insulate pipes in unheated spaces so they do not freeze and split in cold weather.
  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.

Why Emergency Water Damage Restoration Is Not a DIY Job

In an emergency, the hard part is not mopping up what you can see. It is knowing where the rest of the water went. Water runs under baseboards, into wall cavities and beneath flooring within minutes. Meters and thermal imaging show us the wet spots hidden from view, which means they get handled that night rather than surfacing weeks on as a stain or a smell.

A night crew also brings equipment no household has on hand: truck-mounted extractors, commercial air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the space. The crew is trained to stay safe near contaminated water and wet wiring, and from hour one it records the loss in photos and moisture readings. When it comes time to file a claim, that documentation helps. From the first night to a dry house, a single crew stays on your job.
